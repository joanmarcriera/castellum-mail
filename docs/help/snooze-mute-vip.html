Snooze, Mute and VIP senders

Message menu · Castellum

1. Snooze

Snooze hides a message from your Inbox until the time you choose, then brings it back. Select a message and press b (or choose Message > Snooze…).

ChoiceComes back
Later today18:00 today (or in 3 hours if it is already past 18:00)
Tomorrow08:00 tomorrow
This weekendSaturday 09:00
Next weekMonday 08:00
Custom…The date and time you pick

Times use your Mac's clock and time zone. To bring a message back early, open the Snoozed list, select it and press B (Shift-B), or choose Message > Move to Inbox Now.

2. Mute a conversation

Press m to mute the selected conversation, and again to unmute. New replies in a muted conversation arrive already marked read and do not raise a notification. Find muted mail with is:muted.

3. VIP senders

Press v to add the selected message's sender as a VIP, and again to remove them. VIPs let you narrow what interrupts you: in Settings > Notifications you can show banners for VIPs only, and set the Dock badge to count only unread VIP mail. You can also add an address by hand in that tab. Find VIP mail with is:vip.

All the keys are in Keyboard shortcuts.
