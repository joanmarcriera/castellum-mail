Projects board

Projects menu · Castellum

A project gives related mail and people one place to live.

1. The board

Open Projects > Projects Board (⇧⌘P, or g then p). Projects sit in four columns: Active, Waiting, Someday and Done. Right-click a card to Move to another column or to Archive it. Tick Archived at the top to show archived projects. Each card shows how many emails and people are linked.

Click New Project, give it a name, a colour and optional notes.

2. Add mail to a project

Select a message and press p (or choose Projects > Assign to Project). Search for a project and tick it, or choose New Project… to create one on the spot.

3. Inside a project

Click a card to open it. You can rename it, change its status and colour, and edit notes. It lists the linked emails and the people involved. Use Link people to add contacts, and Show network to open the contact graph filtered to this project.

For each link, Why linked shows how it came about: the date, an optional note you wrote, and a link to the message that was the evidence.

4. Delete a project

Delete Project asks you to confirm.
