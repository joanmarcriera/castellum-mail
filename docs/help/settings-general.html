Settings: General

Settings > General · Castellum

One section per heading in Settings > General. Settings for sending, Markdown and key bindings have their own pages: Sending, Composing, Key bindings.

1. Reading

Mark messages read: Never, When opened, or After a delay (1 to 30 seconds, set with the stepper).

2. Layout

Widescreen puts the message list beside the reading pane; Classic puts it above. You can also switch under View in the menu bar.

3. Attachments

Clicking an attachment: Ask each time, or always Open, Save As… or Quick Look. The gear menu on a message's attachment strip offers the same choices.

4. Remote content

Always load remote images is off by default. While it is off, a message with remote images shows a Load button instead, because loading remote images can tell the sender when you read their mail. Senders you chose to always allow (or always block) are listed below with a Revoke button.

5. Spotlight

Index message subjects and senders in Spotlight is off by default. When on, only the subject and sender become searchable in Spotlight. Message bodies, attachments and HTML content are never indexed, and everything stays on your Mac. Turning it off removes everything Castellum indexed.

6. Updates

Check for updates: Never, At launch or Daily (the default, at most once every 24 hours). A check contacts github.com and sends no identifiers. With Never, Castellum contacts github.com only when you choose Check for Updates… from the app menu. Window > Status shows when the last check ran.

7. Default mail app

Click Make Castellum My Default Mail App… to have Castellum open when you click an email link (mailto:). Castellum never does this on its own: macOS asks you to confirm, and you can switch back any time in System Settings.
